# FinPal: Smart Receipt Tracker with AI Assistant
<img width="1261" height="808" alt="Screenshot 2026-02-01 at 9 28 04 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a862c97e-ce71-44ea-8dc5-fb211b9b3b6f" />


**FinPal** automatically extracts structured data from receipt photos using Azure Document Intelligence and provides a context-aware AI assistant that understands your spending patterns. Ask questions about your expenses, get personalized insights, and visualize your spending with interactive charts—all powered by your actual receipt data.
